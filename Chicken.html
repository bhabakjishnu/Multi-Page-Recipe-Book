Chicken Kosha (চিকেন কষা)

Ingredients

  • 750g Chicken, bone-in
  • 3 Onions, finely sliced
  • 2 Tomatoes, pureed
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • Mustard oil, Bay leaves, Green chilies
  • Turmeric, Red Chili powder, Garam Masala

Instructions

  1. Marinate: Mix the chicken with a pinch of turmeric, salt, and half the ginger-garlic paste for 30 minutes.
  2. Base: Heat mustard oil, add bay leaves, and sauté onions until deeply caramelized.
  3. Spices: Add the remaining ginger-garlic paste and tomato puree. Cook until the oil separates from the masala.
  4. Cook the Chicken: Add the marinated chicken. Sauté on high heat for 5 minutes, then reduce to medium-low and cook until tender and coated in a thick, dark gravy.
  5. Garnish: Finish with a dash of garam masala and fresh green chilies.
