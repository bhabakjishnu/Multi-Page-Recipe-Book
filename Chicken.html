Chicken Kosha (চিকেন কষা)

Ingredients

  • 750g Chicken, bone-in
  • 3 Onions, finely sliced
  • 2 Tomatoes, pureed
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • Mustard oil, Bay leaves, Green chilies
  • Turmeric, Red Chili powder, Garam Masala

Instructions

  1. Marinate: Mix chicken with turmeric, salt and half the ginger-garlic paste. Rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Base: Heat mustard oil and add bay leaves. Sauté onions until deeply caramelized.
  3. Spices: Add ginger-garlic paste and tomato puree. Cook until the oil separates from the masala.
  4. Cook the Chicken: Add chicken and sauté on high heat for 5 minutes. Reduce heat and cook until tender.
  5. Garnish: Finish with garam masala and fresh green chilies.
