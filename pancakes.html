Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter















Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the dry ingredients (flour, sugar).
  2. In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients and stir until just mixed.
  4. Cook on a hot griddle until bubbles form, then flip!
