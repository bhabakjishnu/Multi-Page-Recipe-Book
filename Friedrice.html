Fried Rice (ফ্রাইড রাইস)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice, cooked & cooled
  • 1/2 cup Carrots, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup Beans, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup Cashews and Raisins
  • 2 tbsp Ghee (Clarified Butter)
  • Whole spices (Cardamom, Cloves, Cinnamon)

Instructions

  1. Prep the Rice: Cook the basmati rice so it is about 90% done. Spread it on a tray to cool completely to prevent it from turning mushy.
  2. Fry the Nuts: Heat ghee in a large wok and lightly fry the cashews and raisins until golden. Remove and set aside.
  3. Sauté Vegetables: In the same ghee, add the whole spices. Toss in the carrots and beans, adding a pinch of salt and sugar, and fry until tender but still crisp.
  4. Toss: Gently fold the cooled rice into the vegetables. Add the fried nuts and mix well over low heat.
  5. Serve: Perfect alongside Chicken or Mutton Kosha.
