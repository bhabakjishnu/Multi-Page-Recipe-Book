Khichuri (খিচুরি)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Gobindobhog Rice (or short-grain rice)
  • 1 cup Moong Dal (Roasted)
  • 1 Potato, cubed & 1/2 cup Cauliflower florets
  • 1 tbsp Ginger paste
  • Mustard oil and Ghee
  • Dry red chilies, Bay leaves, Cumin, Turmeric

Instructions

  1. Prep: Dry roast the moong dal until fragrant. Wash the rice and dal together. Lightly fry the potatoes and cauliflower in mustard oil.
  2. Temper: In a large heavy-bottomed pot, heat mustard oil and ghee. Add bay leaves, cumin seeds, and dry red chilies.
  3. Spices: Sauté ginger paste with turmeric and a little cumin powder until fragrant.
  4. Boil: Add the washed rice and dal mixture, pour in enough warm water (about 5-6 cups), and bring to a boil. Add the fried vegetables halfway through cooking.
  5. Simmer: Cook until the rice and dal melt together into a porridge-like consistency. Top with a spoonful of ghee before serving.
