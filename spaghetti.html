Classic Spaghetti Bolognese

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti pasta
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • Parmesan cheese (for garnish)















Instructions

  1. Boil water and cook spaghetti according to package directions.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add marinara sauce to the beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Serve sauce over pasta and top with parmesan cheese.
