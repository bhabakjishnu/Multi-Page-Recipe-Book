Sabjivat (সবজি ভাত)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Rice, washed and soaked
  • Mixed Vegetables (Carrot, Peas, Beans, Potato)
  • 1 Onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp Oil or Ghee
  • Whole Garam Masala, Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Sauté Aromatics: Heat ghee/oil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Drop in whole garam masala (cloves, cardamom, cinnamon) and let them splutter.
  2. Fry Veggies: Add the sliced onions and sauté until translucent. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste followed by all the mixed chopped vegetables. Fry for 3-4 minutes.
  3. Add Rice: Drain the soaked rice and gently fold it into the vegetable mixture, roasting it for a minute to coat the grains.
  4. Cook: Add warm water (double the amount of rice), salt to taste, and bring to a boil. Lower the heat, cover, and let it simmer until the water is absorbed and the rice is fluffy.
  5. Serve: Fluff gently with a fork and serve hot with raita or any savory curry.
