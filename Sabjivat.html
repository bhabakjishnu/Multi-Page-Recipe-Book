Sabjivat (সবজি ভাত)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Rice, washed and soaked
  • Carrot, Peas, Beans and Potato
  • 1 Onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp Oil or Ghee
  • Whole Garam Masala and Salt

Instructions

  1. Sauté Aromatics: Heat ghee or oil and add cloves, cardamom and cinnamon.
  2. Fry Vegetables: Add onions and sauté until translucent. Add ginger-garlic paste and mixed vegetables.
  3. Add Rice: Drain soaked rice and gently fold it into the vegetables. Roast for one minute.
  4. Cook: Add warm water, approximately twice the amount of rice. Add salt and simmer until the rice becomes fluffy.
  5. Serve: Fluff gently with a fork and serve hot with raita or curry.
