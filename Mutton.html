Mutton Kosha (মটন কষা)

Ingredients

  • 500g Mutton
  • 3 large Onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • Mustard oil for cooking
  • Spices: Cumin, Coriander, Turmeric, Red Chili powder
  • Salt and Sugar to taste

Instructions

  1. Marinate: Coat the mutton with yogurt, turmeric, and a little mustard oil. Let it sit for at least 2 hours.
  2. Temper the Oil: Heat mustard oil in a heavy-bottomed pan until it smokes, then add whole spices (cardamom, cinnamon, cloves).
  3. Fry the Base: Add chopped onions and fry until deep golden brown. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell disappears.
  4. Slow Cook (Kosha): Add the marinated mutton and dry spices. Cook uncovered on medium-low heat for about an hour, stirring frequently until the meat is tender and the oil separates.
  5. Serve: Serve piping hot with Fried Rice or Butter Naan!
