Butter Naan (বাটার নান)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups All-Purpose Flour (Maida)
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Baking Soda
  • 2 tbsp Yogurt (Curd)
  • 1 tsp Sugar, 1/2 tsp Salt
  • Warm milk or water for kneading, Butter for brushing

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Dough: Sift the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and sugar together. Add the yogurt and mix.
  2. Knead: Gradually add warm milk/water and knead into a soft, smooth dough. Cover with a damp cloth and let it rest for at least 2 hours.
  3. Roll: Divide the dough into small balls. Roll them out into oval shapes. Apply a little water to one side of the rolled dough.
  4. Cook: Heat a tawa (griddle). Place the water side down on the hot tawa. Once bubbles appear, flip the tawa upside down directly over the flame until it browns.
  5. Butter it up: Remove from heat, brush generously with melted butter, and serve hot!
