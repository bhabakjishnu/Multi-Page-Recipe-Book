Fried Rice (ফ্রাইড রাইস)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice, cooked and cooled
  • 1/2 cup Carrots, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup Beans, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup Cashews and Raisins
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • Cardamom, Cloves and Cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Prep the Rice: Cook basmati rice until about 90% done. Spread it on a tray and cool completely.
  2. Fry the Nuts: Heat ghee and fry cashews and raisins until golden. Remove and set aside.
  3. Sauté Vegetables: Add whole spices, carrots and beans. Fry until tender but still crisp.
  4. Toss: Gently fold cooled rice into the vegetables. Add fried nuts and mix over low heat.
  5. Serve: Serve hot with Chicken Kosha or Mutton Kosha.
