Butter Naan (বাটার নান)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups All-Purpose Flour (Maida)
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Baking Soda
  • 2 tbsp Yogurt
  • 1 tsp Sugar and 1/2 tsp Salt
  • Warm milk or water and Butter

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Dough: Sift flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and sugar. Add yogurt and mix.
  2. Knead: Gradually add warm milk or water. Knead into a soft dough and rest for at least 2 hours.
  3. Roll: Divide dough into small balls and roll into oval shapes. Apply water to one side.
  4. Cook: Place the water-coated side on a hot tawa. Once bubbles appear, cook until browned.
  5. Butter It Up: Remove from heat, brush with melted butter and serve hot.
