Khichuri (খিচুরি)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Gobindobhog Rice
  • 1 cup Roasted Moong Dal
  • 1 Potato and 1/2 cup Cauliflower
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Paste
  • Mustard Oil and Ghee
  • Dry Red Chilies, Bay Leaves, Cumin and Turmeric

Instructions

  1. Prep: Dry roast the moong dal until fragrant. Wash rice and dal together. Lightly fry potatoes and cauliflower.
  2. Temper: Heat mustard oil and ghee. Add bay leaves, cumin and dry red chilies.
  3. Spices: Sauté ginger paste with turmeric and cumin powder.
  4. Boil: Add rice and dal with about 5–6 cups of warm water. Add vegetables halfway through cooking.
  5. Simmer: Cook until the rice and dal become soft and porridge-like. Finish with ghee.
