Mutton Kosha (মটন কষা)

Ingredients

  • 500g Mutton
  • 3 large Onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • Mustard oil for cooking
  • Cumin, Coriander, Turmeric, Red Chili powder
  • Salt and Sugar to taste

Instructions

  1. Marinate: Coat the mutton with yogurt, turmeric and a little mustard oil. Let it rest for at least 2 hours.
  2. Temper the Oil: Heat mustard oil until it smokes slightly. Add cardamom, cinnamon and cloves.
  3. Fry the Base: Add chopped onions and fry until deep golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until fragrant.
  4. Slow Cook: Add the marinated mutton and dry spices. Cook on medium-low heat until tender and the oil separates.
  5. Serve: Serve hot with Fried Rice or Butter Naan.
